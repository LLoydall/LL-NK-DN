# Call transcripts

Four recorded calls, rewritten as anonymised transcripts. Speakers are labelled by role. Small talk is
cut and the wording is tidied; the substance is unchanged.

Nothing here names a fund manager, a firm, an administrator, a person, a location or a portfolio company.

| File | What it covers | Useful for |
|---|---|---|
| `call-1-nav-workflow-review` | A fund manager on why a NAV takes six or seven rounds with their administrator: subsequent events left in with dates rolled forward, side letter fee calculations wrong, no one checking that numbers foot. He now runs the administrator's output through an AI tool before reading it. | The clearest statement of the problem these datasets exist to solve. Start here. |
| `call-2-workflow-walkthrough-with-a-prospect` | Ylookup describing the shape of the work — replacing outsourced providers service by service, a report-extraction job that takes an offshore team two months a year, consolidating across fifteen administrators. | Framing and the proof-of-concept approach. |
| `call-3-introductory-call` | A prospective client on taking over an engineering function and modernising it. | Thin on workflow content. Background only. |
| `call-4-internal-account-coverage` | An internal Ylookup call on account ownership and territory. | **Not a client call.** Commercial strategy rather than workflow. Review before circulating. |

## Two caveats

**These are partial.** The recordings run 30 to 40 minutes each; the retrievable transcript covered
roughly the first third to half. Call 1 is the fullest. Full versions need exporting from the recording
tool directly.

**Call 4 is internal.** It is anonymised, but it is a conversation about how Ylookup divides up its
accounts, not a fund manager describing a workflow. It is included for completeness. Decide
deliberately before it goes to contestants.
